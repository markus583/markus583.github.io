📄 New preprint from my **Thomson Reuters Labs** internship: [Equal Ranking Quality, Different Decisions: Training Order-Consistent LLM Scorers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26762)! We show that LLM scorers with the same ranking quality can still make different decisions, and propose OC-SFT to train order-consistent scorers. Code is available [here](https://github.com/thomsonreuters/presentation-dependence).
